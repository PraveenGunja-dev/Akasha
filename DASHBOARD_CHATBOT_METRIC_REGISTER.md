## Conventions

- Scope filters below describe current implementation, including known inconsistencies; they are not recommendations.
- `Business cutoff` means the source-effective timestamp, such as P6 `data_date`. `Sync` means ingestion time, such as `last_synced_at` or `upload_time`.
- Dashboard `/api/dashboard/summary`, `/api/financials`, and `/api/logistics` can serve process-local cached output for 300 seconds. Use `nocache=true` when freezing those baselines. Capacity and quality routes have no route cache in the reviewed code.
- Unless stated otherwise, counts are integers and identifiers/statuses are exact strings.
- Parity statuses: `Aligned` means current structured values are expected to match; `Different` means known formula/scope/rounding differences; `No counterpart` means the chatbot does not expose that dashboard fact; `Frontend-only` means the authoritative calculation currently executes in React.

## Missing-P6 Compatibility Decision

The missing-P6 schedule-status display decision is approved:

- Keep the mapping in `portfolio.project_count`.
- Return independently matched capacity, SAP, TC, and Pulse facts.
- Leave P6-derived progress, dates, variance, delay, SPI/CPI, critical path, and P6 milestones unavailable/null in new shared facts.
- Do not infer schedule health from mapping or another source.
- Preserve the dashboard's `On Track` and progress `0` fallback through its compatibility adapter, including the existing on-track aggregate behavior.
- Do not treat that fallback as the authoritative schedule formula or P6 evidence.
