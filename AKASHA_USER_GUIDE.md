## 1. What is Akasha?

Akasha is a unified project analytics and intelligence platform built for Adani Green Energy Limited. It brings together data from multiple systems — including P6 (project scheduling), SAP (financials), Transmission data, and more — into a single, easy-to-use dashboard.

**What can you do with Akasha?**

- **Monitor all projects at a glance** — See the health, progress, and financial status of every project in your portfolio.
- **Drill into any project** — Open a detailed workspace for any individual project to see schedules, costs, risks, and work breakdown structures.
- **Talk to Akasha's AI assistant** — Ask questions in plain English about your projects and get instant, data-backed answers with charts and visualizations.
- **Generate reports** — Create and download executive-level reports with one click.
- **Track risks** — Identify and monitor project risks with a heat map and risk scoring system.
- **Run simulations** — Use the Simulation Lab to explore "what-if" scenarios and understand how changes could impact your projects.
- **Stay informed** — Receive notifications about project milestones, delays, and important updates.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Opening the Platform

Open your web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox recommended) and navigate to the Akasha URL provided by your IT team. The address typically looks like:

```
https://your-company-domain/akasha
```

You will see the Akasha landing page with the platform name displayed prominently, along with a **Login** button and a **Documentation** button.

### 2.2 Logging In

1. Click the **Login** button on the landing page.
2. A sign-in window will appear.
3. Click **Sign in with Microsoft** — this will use your company Microsoft account to authenticate you.
4. Once authenticated, you will be automatically redirected to your dashboard based on your assigned role.

> **Note:** Your role (CEO/Executive or PMAG) is determined by your organization's IT administrator through Microsoft Entra (Azure AD). You do not need to choose your role manually.

### 2.3 Understanding Your Role

Akasha has two primary user roles, each with a tailored dashboard experience:

| Role | Who is it for? | What do you see? |
|------|---------------|------------------|
| **Executive** | C-Suite leaders, senior management | Full CEO Dashboard with all modules — Overview, Project 360, Risk, Financials, AI tools, Reports, Admin, and more |
| **PMAG** | Portfolio Management & Governance team | PMAG Dashboard focused on portfolio tracking, site monitoring, grid status, team management, and field-level insights |

Both roles have access to the **Ask Akasha** AI assistant, Reports, Smart Search, and other intelligence features.

## 3. Navigating the Platform

Once you log in, the platform has three main areas:

### 3.1 Sidebar Navigation

On the **left side** of the screen, you will find the navigation sidebar. This is your main menu for moving between different sections of the platform.

**For Executive Users**, the sidebar is organized into three groups:

- **Dashboard** — Overview, Capacity Overview, Project 360, Quality
- **AI & Intelligence** — Ask Akasha, Briefing, Search, Project Map, Knowledge Graph, Simulation
- **Administration** — Reports, Admin

**For PMAG Users**, the sidebar groups are:

- **Dashboard** — Portfolio, Site Monitoring, Grid Status
- **Data & Insights** — Briefing, Search, Project Map
- **Administration** — Reports, Team

**Tips:**
- Click on any item in the sidebar to switch to that section.
- You can **collapse the sidebar** by clicking the collapse arrow at the bottom to give yourself more screen space. Hover over the icons to see tooltips when collapsed.
- On mobile devices, tap the **hamburger menu** (☰) in the top-left corner to open the sidebar.

### 3.2 Top Header Bar

The top bar runs across the top of the dashboard and provides quick-access controls:

- **Dashboard Title** — Shows which dashboard you are currently viewing.
- **Portfolio Filter** — A dropdown to filter your data by a specific portfolio (e.g., Solar, Wind, or All Portfolios).
- **Project Filter** — Select a specific project to focus all dashboard data on that project, or choose "All" to see everything.
- **Sync All Data** button — Refreshes all data from source systems (P6, SAP, SharePoint, etc.). More details in the [Data Sync](#8-data-sync) section.
- **Ask Akasha** button — Opens the AI assistant in a small floating panel on the right side of the screen.
- **Theme Toggle** (☀️/🌙) — Switch between light and dark mode.
- **Notifications Bell** (🔔) — Shows your unread notifications. A red dot appears when you have new alerts.
- **User Profile** — Shows your name and role. Hover to see the sign-out option.

### 3.3 Switching Between Light and Dark Mode

Akasha supports both light and dark themes for comfortable viewing in any environment.

- Click the **sun/moon icon** in the top-right corner of the header bar.
- The entire interface will instantly switch themes.
- Your preference is applied for the current session.

## 4. CEO / Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard is a comprehensive command center designed for senior leadership to monitor the entire project portfolio at a glance.

### 4.1 Executive Overview

**What it shows:** This is your home screen — a high-level snapshot of the entire portfolio.

**Key information displayed:**
- **KPI Cards** at the top showing critical numbers:
  - Total projects in portfolio
  - Total capacity (in MW)
  - Delayed projects count
  - On-track projects count
  - Average progress percentage
  - Total PO (Purchase Order) value
- **AI Briefing Panel** — An AI-generated summary of the portfolio's current state, key risks, and highlights. This updates automatically based on real data.
- **Project Ranking Lists** — Tabs to view top-performing projects, lowest-performing projects, and delayed projects.
- **Charts** — Visual representations of portfolio performance, progress distribution, and trends.

**How to use it:**
- Click on any **KPI card** to see a detailed breakdown of that metric.
- Read the **AI Briefing** panel for a quick executive summary without having to dig through data manually.
- Use the project ranking tabs to quickly identify which projects need attention.

### 4.2 Capacity Overview

**What it shows:** A detailed view of the energy generation capacity across all projects, broken down by solar and wind, and by milestone stage (Trial Run vs. COD — Commercial Operation Date).

**Key information displayed:**
- Total Solar COD capacity (MW)
- Total Wind COD capacity (MW)
- Trial Run capacity (MW)
- Capacity breakdown by financial year (FY)
- Recent milestone achievements
- Project-level capacity breakdown table

**How to use it:**
- Review the KPI cards at the top for a quick capacity summary.
- Use the financial year chart to understand how capacity is distributed over time.
- Scroll down to the project breakdown table to see individual project capacity details.

### 4.3 Project 360

**What it shows:** A complete view of every project in your portfolio, organized by health status.

**Key information displayed:**
- **Status pills** at the top showing counts by health category: Critical, High Risk, Watchlist, Healthy, and Completed.
- **Project cards** for each project showing: progress percentage, SPI (Schedule Performance Index), capacity, key issues, and risk flags.
- **Search bar** to find specific projects by name.
- **Risk filter** dropdown to filter projects by risk type (Material Risk, Schedule Risk, Vendor Risk, etc.).

**How to use it:**
- Click on a **status pill** (e.g., "Critical") to filter and show only projects in that health category.
- Click on any **project card** to open the detailed [Project Workspace](#7-project-workspace) for that project.
- Use the **search bar** at the top to quickly find a project by name.
- Use the **risk filter** to narrow down projects with a specific type of risk.

### 4.4 Quality Command Center

**What it shows:** An overview of quality metrics across all projects, focused on Non-Conformance (NC) management.

**Key information displayed:**
- Total NCs raised, resolved, and pending
- NC workflow pipeline showing how many NCs are at each stage (Raised → In Review by Execution Engineer → In Review by Quality Inspector → Approved/Rejected)
- Contractor performance breakdown
- NC trend charts over time
- Filterable NC list with search

**How to use it:**
- Review the KPI cards for a quick quality summary.
- Use the **workflow visualization** to see where NCs are bottlenecked in the approval process.
- Click on the **filter tabs** to show only NCs in a specific status (e.g., Raised, In Review, Approved).
- Use the **search bar** to find specific NCs by project name, contractor, or description.

### 4.5 Portfolio Health

**What it shows:** An aggregated health view combining schedule and logistics data to show the overall well-being of the portfolio.

**Key information displayed:**
- Project health status breakdown
- Schedule variance trends
- Logistics and delivery status

**How to use it:**
- Use this section to quickly identify which projects are falling behind schedule or facing logistics challenges.

### 4.6 Schedule (P6 View)

**What it shows:** Detailed scheduling data pulled from the P6 scheduling system.

**Key information displayed:**
- Schedule Performance Index (SPI) for each project
- Duration percent complete
- Baseline vs. actual finish dates
- Critical path activities
- Milestones and their status

**How to use it:**
- Review the data table to compare planned vs. actual schedules for each project.
- Look at the SPI values — a value below 1.0 indicates the project is behind schedule.

### 4.7 Financial (SAP View)

**What it shows:** Financial data from SAP, including purchase orders, costs, and logistics information.

**Key information displayed:**
- PO (Purchase Order) summaries
- Financial aggregates
- Cost breakdowns
- Logistics delivery data
- Vendor-level financial details

**How to use it:**
- Review PO values and delivery status to understand the financial health of your projects.
- Use the financial details to track spending against budget.

### 4.8 Procurement Intelligence

**What it shows:** Detailed insights into procurement activity, vendor performance, and purchase order analytics.

**How to use it:**
- Review vendor-level PO data to identify top suppliers and potential procurement bottlenecks.

### 4.9 Material Intelligence

**What it shows:** Insights into material logistics, delivery tracking, and material supply chain status.

**How to use it:**
- Monitor material delivery status and identify any supply chain delays that could impact project timelines.

### 4.10 Transmission Data

**What it shows:** Data related to the transmission infrastructure (substations, transmission lines, connectivity).

**How to use it:**
- View transmission project statuses and connectivity data.
- Track evacuation infrastructure readiness alongside generation project progress.

### 4.11 Risk Command Center

**What it shows:** A comprehensive risk analysis module with a visual heat map and risk scoring.

**Key information displayed:**
- Overall risk score for the portfolio
- Schedule risk count and details
- Financial risk count and details
- **Risk Heat Map** — a visual chart plotting risks by probability (how likely) vs. impact (how severe), helping you focus on the most critical risks first.

**How to use it:**
- Review the **overall risk score** to get a quick pulse on portfolio risk.
- Look at the **heat map** — risks in the top-right corner (high probability + high impact) need the most urgent attention.
- Click on individual risk entries to see supporting evidence and details.

### 4.12 Predictive Analytics

**What it shows:** AI-powered predictions for project outcomes based on current performance data.

**How to use it:**
- Review predicted completion dates and cost forecasts.
- Use the predictions to anticipate issues before they become critical.

### 4.13 Decision Center

**What it shows:** A curated view of key decisions needed across the portfolio, combining schedule and financial data.

**How to use it:**
- Use this module to prepare for review meetings by seeing what decisions are pending.
- Review flagged items that require executive attention.

### 4.14 Reports & Insights

**What it shows:** A reporting module that generates downloadable reports based on your project data.

**Key information displayed:**
- Portfolio progress summary
- Financial highlights
- Top vendors by PO value
- Schedule performance metrics
- Downloadable report options

**How to use it:**
- Review the on-screen report summary for a quick overview.
- Click the **Download** button to export the report for sharing with stakeholders.
- Reports combine data from P6 (schedule), SAP (financials), and the dashboard summary into a unified format.

## 6. AI-Powered Features

Akasha includes several artificial intelligence features designed to help you get insights faster without needing to manually dig through data.

### 6.1 Ask Akasha (AI Copilot)

This is Akasha's built-in AI conversational assistant. Think of it as having a personal analyst who has access to all your project data and can answer your questions instantly.

**How to open it:**
- **Full-screen mode:** Click **"Ask Akasha"** in the left sidebar (Executive Dashboard) to open it as a dedicated full-screen experience.
- **Floating panel:** Click the **"Ask Akasha"** button in the top header bar to open it as a compact panel on the right side of the screen — useful when you want to ask a quick question without leaving your current view.

**What you can do:**
- **Ask questions in plain English.** For example:
  - *"Which projects are delayed?"*
  - *"What is the total capacity of our solar projects?"*
  - *"Show me the financial summary for Khavda project"*
  - *"How many milestones are overdue this month?"*
  - *"What are the top risks in my portfolio?"*
- **Get visualizations.** Akasha can generate charts and graphs directly in the chat to help you visualize the data. These appear inline with the AI's response.
- **Follow-up questions.** You can have a natural back-and-forth conversation. Ask follow-up questions to drill deeper into a topic.
- **Voice input.** Click the **microphone icon** to speak your question instead of typing it.
- **Attach images.** Click the **paperclip icon** to attach an image for the AI to analyze.

**Managing your conversations:**
- **New conversation:** Click the **"+"** button to start a fresh conversation thread.
- **Chat history:** Click the **history icon** to see your previous conversations. Click on any past conversation to continue it.
- **Rename a conversation:** Click the **pencil icon** next to a conversation title to give it a meaningful name.
- **Delete a conversation:** Click the **trash icon** to remove a conversation from your history.

**Giving feedback:**
- After the AI responds, you will see **thumbs up** (👍) and **thumbs down** (👎) icons. Use these to rate the response — this helps improve Akasha's accuracy over time.

**Stopping a response:**
- If Akasha is generating a long response, you can click the **Stop** button to cancel it mid-stream.

**Maximizing and minimizing:**
- When using the floating panel, click the **expand icon** to switch to full-screen mode.
- When in full-screen mode, click the **minimize icon** to go back to the floating panel.

### 6.2 Executive Briefing

**What it is:** An AI-generated executive summary that gives you a concise, narrative overview of your portfolio's current state.

**How to access it:**
- Click **"Briefing"** in the left sidebar.

**What you get:**
- A written briefing covering key metrics, risks, highlights, and action items.
- The briefing is generated by AI based on real-time data from all connected systems.
- It is designed to save you time by summarizing the most important information you need to know.

### 6.3 Smart Search

**What it is:** A powerful search engine that lets you find any project, purchase order, material component, or vendor across the entire Akasha platform.

**How to access it:**
- Click **"Search"** in the left sidebar.

**How to use it:**
1. Type your search query in the search bar (e.g., a project name, vendor name, PO number, or material type).
2. Press **Enter** or click the search button.
3. Results will appear below, categorized by type (Project, Purchase Order, Material Component, Vendor).
4. Click on any result to navigate to its detailed view.

### 6.4 Project Map

**What it is:** An interactive geographical map showing all projects plotted at their physical locations across India.

**How to access it:**
- Click **"Project Map"** in the left sidebar.

**How to use it:**
- Browse the map to see where projects are located.
- Click on a project marker to see a quick summary of that project.
- Click to open the full Project Workspace for that project.

### 6.5 Knowledge Graph

**What it is:** A visual, interactive network diagram that shows how projects, portfolios, vendors, and other entities are connected to each other.

**How to access it:**
- Click **"Knowledge Graph"** in the left sidebar (Executive Dashboard only).

**How to use it:**
- The graph starts with a central node representing the organization.
- Click on any node to **expand it** and see its connected entities (e.g., click on a portfolio to see its projects, click on a project to see its vendors and milestones).
- Click on an expanded node to **collapse** it.
- Use the **search bar** at the top to find and highlight a specific entity.
- Use the **filter** controls to show or hide specific types of entities.
- Zoom in and out using your mouse scroll wheel or pinch gestures.

### 6.6 Simulation Lab

**What it is:** A "what-if" scenario analysis tool that lets you model different scenarios and see their potential impact on project outcomes.

**How to access it:**
- Click **"Simulation"** in the left sidebar (Executive Dashboard only).

**How to use it:**
1. Select a project from the list on the left.
2. The system will scan the project's current risk profile.
3. Adjust scenario parameters (e.g., weather delays, resource changes, timeline shifts).
4. Review the simulated impact on schedule, cost, and risk.
5. The AI will provide recommendations for mitigating identified risks.

## 7. Project Workspace

The Project Workspace is a detailed, project-level view that gives you a 360-degree look at a single project. You can open it from several places in the platform:

- Click on a project name in the **Project 360** module.
- Click on a project in the **Smart Search** results.
- Click on a project marker in the **Project Map**.

**What the workspace includes:**

- **Project header** — Project name, health status, capacity, and key identifiers.
- **Schedule tab** — Detailed schedule information from P6 including milestones, activities, and Gantt chart views.
- **Financial tab** — SAP financial data including purchase orders, costs, and vendor details.
- **Quality tab** — Non-conformance data specific to this project.
- **WBS (Work Breakdown Structure)** — A hierarchical breakdown of the project's activities and tasks.
- **Risk & Analytics** — Project-specific risk assessment and performance analytics.

**How to return to the main dashboard:**
- Click the **Back** button (←) at the top of the workspace to return to the main dashboard.

## 8. Data Sync

Akasha integrates data from multiple source systems. To ensure you are viewing the latest information:

1. Click the **"Sync All Data"** button in the top header bar.
2. The system will fetch the latest data from all connected sources:
   - **SharePoint** — Document and data repositories
   - **P6** — Project scheduling data
   - **SAP** — Financial and procurement data
   - **Transmission** — Grid and connectivity data
   - **Capacity** — Milestone and capacity data
   - **Pulse** — Operational data
3. A spinning icon will appear while syncing is in progress.
4. Once complete, all dashboard data will automatically refresh.

> **Note:** Data sync may take a few moments depending on the volume of data. You can continue using the platform while the sync is running.

## 10. Admin Panel

The Admin Panel is available to Executive users and provides system management tools.

**How to access it:**
- Click **"Admin"** in the left sidebar (Executive Dashboard).

### 10.1 Project Mappings

This is the primary admin function currently available. Project Mappings allow administrators to align data from different source systems (Transmission, P6, and SAP) so that Akasha can correctly combine data for each project.

**What you can do:**
- **View all mappings** — See a table of all currently configured project mappings.
- **Search mappings** — Use the search bar to find a specific mapping by project name or SPV name.
- **Create a new mapping** — Click **"New Mapping"** to open the mapping form, then fill in:
  - Transmission Project Name
  - P6 Project Name
  - P6 Project ID
  - SPV Name
  - SPV Plant Code
  - Capacity (MW AC)
- **Edit a mapping** — Click the **pencil icon** on any row to modify it.
- **Delete a mapping** — Click the **trash icon** on any row to remove it (you will be asked to confirm).

> **Important:** Project mappings are critical for data accuracy. Only make changes if you understand how the data sources should be linked. Contact your system administrator if you are unsure.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: I can't log in. What should I do?**
A: Make sure you are using your company Microsoft account. If you still cannot log in, contact your IT administrator to ensure your account has the correct role assigned in Microsoft Entra (Azure AD).

**Q: The dashboard shows "Loading..." and never finishes. What's wrong?**
A: Try refreshing the page in your browser (press F5 or Ctrl+R). If the issue persists, click the **"Sync All Data"** button to trigger a fresh data pull. If it still doesn't load, check your internet connection or contact IT support.

**Q: The data on my dashboard seems outdated. How do I get the latest numbers?**
A: Click the **"Sync All Data"** button in the top header bar. This will pull the latest data from all source systems (P6, SAP, SharePoint, etc.).

**Q: I asked Akasha a question but the answer seems wrong. What should I do?**
A: Click the **thumbs down** (👎) button on the response to flag it. You can also try rephrasing your question for better results. The AI improves over time based on feedback.

**Q: Can I use Akasha on my phone or tablet?**
A: Yes. Akasha is designed to work on mobile devices. The sidebar will collapse into a hamburger menu (☰) on smaller screens. However, for the best experience, we recommend using a desktop or laptop computer.

## 12. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **COD** | Commercial Operation Date — the date a project begins commercial energy generation |
| **CPI** | Cost Performance Index — a measure of cost efficiency (1.0 = on budget, <1.0 = over budget) |
| **DPR** | Daily Progress Report |
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator — a measurable value that shows how well something is performing |
| **MW / MWac / MWdc** | Megawatt — a unit of electrical power. MWac = alternating current, MWdc = direct current |
| **GW** | Gigawatt — 1,000 Megawatts |
| **NC** | Non-Conformance — a quality deviation that doesn't meet the required standard |
| **P6** | Primavera P6 — an enterprise project scheduling software |
| **PMAG** | Portfolio Management & Governance |
| **PO** | Purchase Order |
| **SAP** | Systems, Applications and Products — an enterprise resource planning (ERP) system used for financials |
| **SPI** | Schedule Performance Index — a measure of schedule efficiency (1.0 = on schedule, <1.0 = behind schedule) |
| **SPV** | Special Purpose Vehicle — a legal entity created for a specific project |
| **Trial Run** | A pre-COD testing phase where the plant generates power to verify systems work correctly |
| **WBS** | Work Breakdown Structure — a hierarchical breakdown of project tasks and deliverables |
